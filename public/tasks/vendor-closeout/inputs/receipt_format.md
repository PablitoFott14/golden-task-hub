## Template

```
VENDOR CLOSEOUT RECEIPT
Harmony Games, Inc.

Vendor:              <vendor name>
Service:             <what the vendor provided, one line>
Cancellation date:   <YYYY-MM-DD>
Confirmed by:        <name of the person who confirmed it>
Final amount due:    <amount, or "None outstanding">
```

---

## Field rules

**Vendor** - spell it exactly as it appears in our internal records, including
capitalisation. Do not tidy it up. Only real vendor names.

**Service** - one short line describing what they provided. No more than a 30 words.

**Cancellation date** - the date the cancellation was confirmed in the slack channel. Format `YYYY-MM-DD` (PST).

**Confirmed by** - full name of the person whose Slack message confirms or provides the latests evidence that the vendor was cancelled.

**Final amount due** - the outstanding balance owed at closure. with the currency symbol and two decimals, for example `$1,687.50`. If nothing is owed or no evidence exists, write `None outstanding`.
