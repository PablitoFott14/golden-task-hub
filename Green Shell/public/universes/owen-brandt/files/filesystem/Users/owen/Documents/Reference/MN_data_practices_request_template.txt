MINNESOTA DATA PRACTICES REQUEST (template)
Adapted from SEJ's sample state letter, June 2026. Replace everything in parentheses.

---

Date:

To: (responsible authority or data practices compliance official, by name if the agency's data access policy lists one)
(Agency name, mailing address or email)

Re: Request for public government data

This is a request under the Minnesota Government Data Practices Act, Minn. Stat. ch. 13, for copies of the following public government data:

(Describe the records: what they are, the office that keeps them, the date range. One subject per request.)

Please provide the data electronically, in the format in which they are maintained. For spreadsheets and databases, I request the native file or an export (.xlsx or .csv) rather than a PDF.

If there will be a charge, please send me a written estimate before doing any work that would cost more than $25. I understand that inspection of public data is free and that the agency may not charge for time spent separating public from not-public data.

If any data are withheld or redacted, please identify them and cite the specific statute or classification that applies, as Minn. Stat. 13.03 requires.

I'm glad to narrow this request if that would speed things up. Please call or email with any questions.

Thank you,

(Name)
(Outlet, or freelance)
(Phone and email)

---

Notes for writers (not part of the letter)
- Minn. Stat. 13.05, subd. 12: an agency can't require you to identify yourself or explain why you want public data. Contact details are there only so they can send the records.
- No fixed deadline for public data. Log the date sent. First follow-up at 10 business days, and ask for a completion date in writing.
- After two missed dates, ask the Data Practices Office (Dept. of Administration) for an advisory opinion.
- Partial releases are fine. Ask for what's ready now and the rest on a rolling basis.
- Inspection is free. If an estimate is high, offer to inspect in person and copy only what you need.
