## Action potential, the way Dr. Silva does it on the board
1. Resting membrane potential about -70 mV
2. Stimulus opens voltage-gated Na+ channels, Na+ flows in
3. Threshold about -55 mV, depolarization up to about +30 mV
4. Na+ channels inactivate, voltage-gated K+ channels open, K+ flows out (repolarization)
5. K+ channels close slowly, so the membrane hyperpolarizes
6. Na+/K+ pump keeps the gradients (3 Na+ out, 2 K+ in)

## Special senses
- Rods: dim light. Cones: color, packed in the fovea centralis
- Light path: cornea → aqueous humor → lens → vitreous humor → retina
- Sound path: tympanic membrane → malleus → incus → stapes → oval window → cochlea (spiral organ)
- Near vision: ciliary muscle contracts, lens gets rounder
